# Install on Android

_Install the HyperWheel Player app on an Android phone or tablet by downloading the APK._

> **Tested on:** an Android 14 phone emulator. The app has not yet been tested on a physical phone or tablet. The Android prompts below vary a little between makers and versions.

## What you need

- an Android phone or tablet running Android 7 or later
- an internet connection

The app is not in the Google Play Store, so you install it from a file (an APK). Android calls this installing from an unknown source.

## Download

On the device, open the **Player apps** page in HyperWheel and download the Android file. Or open **hypr.onl/get** on the device, which starts the download. You can also download `HyperWheel-Player_latest_android.apk` from the [releases page](https://github.com/AstrohubPty/HyperWheelPlayers/releases/latest) on a computer and copy it across.

## Install

1. Open the downloaded file from your browser's downloads or from the **Files** app.
2. Android asks you to allow installing from this source. Select **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source** for your browser or Files app, then go back.
3. Select **Install**.
4. Select **Open**.

On Android 7, open **Settings**, then **Security**, and turn on **Unknown sources** first.

## First launch

The app opens full screen and shows **Link this screen**. Follow [Connect a device](https://docs.hyperwheel.app/screens/connect-a-device/) from the QR code step. To open the settings panel, swipe from the left edge of the screen, or connect a keyboard and press **F2**.

## Start on boot

**Launch on boot** is on by default. On Android 10 and later it also needs **Display over other apps**. Open the settings panel and look under **Settings**: the **Display over other apps** row shows **Not allowed** until you allow it. Select it and Android opens the right screen. Turn on **HyperWheel Player**, then go back.

## Update

The app checks for a new version every few hours. When one is ready, **Actions** in the settings panel shows a dot. Open **Actions**, select **Install**, and confirm in the Android prompt. The first time, Android also asks you to allow **Install unknown apps** for HyperWheel Player.

## Uninstall

Press and hold the app icon, select **App info**, then **Uninstall**.

## Troubleshooting

- **App not installed.** The phone may not have enough free space, or Android may be older than version 7.
- **I cannot find the download.** Open the **Downloads** app, or your browser's downloads list.
- **The app does not start after a restart.** Allow **Display over other apps**, as above.
- **The screen stays on Link this screen.** See [Connect a device](https://docs.hyperwheel.app/screens/connect-a-device/).
